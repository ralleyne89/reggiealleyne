- article:
  - paragraph: Audience hypothesis
  - heading "Dog owner looking for local connection beyond another swipe app." [level=3]
  - paragraph: They want their dog to be part of their social life, from walks and meetups to shared dog-friendly places.
  - term: Working need
  - definition: Profiles, messaging, and local discovery that make the product feel like a dog-centered community rather than a novelty.
  - term: Risk to test
  - definition: A dating-style interface can feel shallow if it does not give users a reason to keep engaging after the first match.

  - paragraph: Evidence boundary

- article:
  - paragraph: Workflow to validate
  - list:
    - text: "01"
    - heading "Create the pair profile" [level=4]
    - paragraph: Set up identity for both owner and dog so matches have more context than a single photo.
    - text: "02"
    - heading "Browse nearby matches" [level=4]
    - paragraph: Scan local people, dogs, and interests through a familiar discovery pattern.

    - text: "04"
    - heading "Build local habit" [level=4]
    - paragraph: Return for parks, places, and community connections that keep the app useful after matching.
