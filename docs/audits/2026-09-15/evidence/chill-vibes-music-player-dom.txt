- article:
  - paragraph: Audience hypothesis
  - heading "Listener who wants a calm player focused on the music itself." [level=3]
  - paragraph: They are tired of music apps that turn listening into another busy social or recommendation surface.
  - term: Working need
  - definition: A responsive MVP that keeps core playback clear enough for client validation and user testing.
  - term: Risk to test
  - definition: Large streaming products often bury playback behind feeds, menus, social layers, and competing actions.

  - paragraph: Evidence boundary
  - paragraph: The available project archive does not preserve participant counts, interview notes, or a research synthesis for this audience model. Treat it as a design hypothesis to test, not a measured persona.

- article:
  - paragraph: Workflow to validate
  - list:
    - text: "01"
    - heading "Open the player" [level=4]
    - paragraph: Arrive at a focused interface where playback is the main task, not a secondary action.
    - text: "02"
    - heading "Choose music" [level=4]
    - paragraph: Browse enough content to start listening without navigating through a heavy media library.
    - text: "03"
    - heading "Control playback" [level=4]
    - paragraph: Use clear controls and responsive layout to keep the session comfortable across devices.
    - text: "04"
    - heading "Return to listen" [level=4]
    - paragraph: Come back for a simple listening experience that does not ask for social engagement first.
