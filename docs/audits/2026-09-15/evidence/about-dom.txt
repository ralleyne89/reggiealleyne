- heading "How I think about AI tools" [level=2]
- heading "Building AI tools people can question" [level=3]
- paragraph: "Most AI interfaces ask people to trust an answer without showing how the system got there. That is a product problem: people cannot rely on a tool they cannot inspect."
- paragraph: I design for algorithmic transparency. When an AI makes a recommendation, people should see the reasoning, the uncertainty, and the inputs that shaped the answer. When it needs more data, it should ask instead of guessing.
- paragraph: The goal is not to make AI feel friendly. It is to make the system legible, so people know when to trust it, challenge it, or override it.
